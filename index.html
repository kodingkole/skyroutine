PC & Mobile App & Wallpaper Setup Guide

How to keep SkyRoutine open 24/7 on PC & Phone

🖥️ 1. How to Install as PC Desktop App:
  1. Click the "📲 Install App" button at top bar.
  2. Click Install when Chrome/Edge asks.
  3. A shortcut with Kiwi Cat icon will be created on your Desktop & Taskbar! Double-click to launch anytime!
🖼️ 2. How to set as Active Live PC Wallpaper Widget:
  1. Click "🖥️ Wallpaper Mode" in top nav bar to hide navigation.
  2. Use free Lively Wallpaper app (from Microsoft Store) & paste your link http://localhost:8080 as your live interactive desktop wallpaper!
📱 3. How to install on Android & iPhone:
  1. Open https://kodingkole.github.io/skyroutine/ in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. iPhone: Tap Share Button ➔ Tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Android: Tap 3 dots (⋮) ➔ Tap "Install App".
